Questions

When is Black Friday {{ year }}?

{{ day(blackFridayDate) }}, the day after Thanksgiving. Cyber Monday follows on {{ day(cyberMondayDate) }}. Many stores start their sales several days early and run them through the weekend.

How can I tell if a discount is real?

Look at what the product actually sold for recently, not at the crossed-out price. This page compares today's price with the lowest price in the 30 days before the sale started; that is the rule EU law sets for "was" prices, and we apply it to every store we track. Our product pages show the full price history.

Do discount codes count?

No. We measure the price shown on the store's product page; coupon codes and cart deals can take more off.

How is this different from the "Real discount" label on the site?

The label on product cards uses a looser rule: it compares today's price with the usual price we saw on at least 7 different days in the last 30. This page uses the stricter 30-day-low test.

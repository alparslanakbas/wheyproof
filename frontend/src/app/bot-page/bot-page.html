wheyproofbot

wheyproofbot is the crawler behind {{ siteName }}. It reads public product prices from supplement stores so we can show each product's price history and send shoppers to the store that sells it.

What it fetches

Public pages only: a store's product catalog (on Shopify stores, /products.json), its home page to confirm the currency, and individual product pages for ratings and nutrition facts. It never logs in and never touches carts, checkouts or accounts.

How often

Each store's catalog about four times a day, one request at a time with a pause between pages. Across every store we read, that is about a thousand requests a day.

robots.txt and how to block it

wheyproofbot reads each store's robots.txt at least once a day and follows the group named wheyproofbot, or the * group if there is none. To stop it reading your store: 

User-agent: wheyproofbot
Disallow: /

What we keep

Prices, product names, sizes, stock status, images and nutrition facts, stored on our own server and shown on our sites with a link back to the store. We don't sell or pass on the data.

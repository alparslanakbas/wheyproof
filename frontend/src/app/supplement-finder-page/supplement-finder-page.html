The quiz asks no health questions and isn’t personal medical advice. If you have a health condition, take medication, or are pregnant or breastfeeding, talk to your doctor before starting a supplement.

New to training?

Most early progress comes from training consistently and eating enough; supplements replace neither. If you want to start with something, two are enough: creatine monohydrate (3–5 g a day) and, if you can’t reach your protein target from food, protein powder.

Pre-workouts, BCAAs and fat burners shouldn’t be your first purchase: the first two have small effects, and fat burners haven’t been shown to cause meaningful fat loss without a calorie deficit.

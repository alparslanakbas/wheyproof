We don't use cookies right now

WheyProof uses no cookies for advertising or tracking, and no visitor analytics. Your theme, cookie consent and watchlist token are kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage); the theme and consent choices never leave your device, and the watchlist token is only sent when you open your own watchlist.

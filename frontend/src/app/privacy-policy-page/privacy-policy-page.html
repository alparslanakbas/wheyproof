01

Who We Are

WheyProof (wheyproof.com) is an independent price-tracking site for sports supplements. This policy covers the data collected on this site. For questions, email us at support@wheyproof.com.

02

What Data We Collect

  • Email newsletter: when you subscribe, we store your email address and the date you subscribed. We send nothing until you click the confirmation link (double opt-in).
  • Price alerts: when you watch a product, we store your email address together with that product. When the price drops we send one alert and close the request.
  • Watchlist: your watchlist is tied to a random token stored in your browser. If you ask for a recovery link, we use your email address only to send it.
  • Theme choice: your light/dark mode choice is kept only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and is never sent to us.
  • Click counter: we count clicks on "Go to store" links per product, as a number that isn't tied to any person.
  • Security log: we record attacks and abuse attempts against the site: IP address, date/time, the requested address and the response code. Normal browsing is not recorded; only unauthorized access attempts, requests over the rate limit, known vulnerability scans and server errors are. Records are deleted automatically after 90 days, are never used for marketing and are not shared with third parties, except with the appropriate authorities if we report abuse.

There is currently no advertising or visitor analytics on this site. If we add any, this page will be updated first, and anything that uses cookies will ask for your consent before it runs. See the Cookie Policy for details.

03

How We Use Data

We use your email address only to send the newsletter you subscribed to and the price alerts you asked for. We use an email service provider to deliver them; your address goes to that provider for this purpose only. We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Some links to stores may be affiliate links. When you click one and leave our site, the store's own privacy policy and cookies apply there.

04

Retention and Deletion

We keep subscription data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every newsletter and alert email has an "unsubscribe" link that deactivates your record immediately. To have your data deleted completely, just write to support@wheyproof.com.

05

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California and other US states with privacy laws), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We don't sell or share personal information, but you can make any of these requests, whatever state you live in, by emailing support@wheyproof.com. We won't treat you differently for making a request.

06

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us their email address, contact us and we'll delete it.

02

What Data We Collect

  • Email newsletter: when you subscribe, we store your email address and the date you subscribed. We send nothing until you click the confirmation link (double opt-in).
  • Price alerts: when you watch a product, we store your email address together with that product. When the price drops we send one alert and close the request.
  • Watchlist: your watchlist is tied to a random token stored in your browser. If you ask for a recovery link, we use your email address only to send it.
  • Theme choice: your light/dark mode choice is kept only in your browser's local storage (localStorage) and is never sent to us.
  • Click counter: we count clicks on "Go to store" links per product, as a number that isn't tied to any person.
  • Server logs: like most websites, our web server writes a line for each request it receives: IP address, date and time, the page address requested, the response code, your browser's user agent and the country our network provider reports for the request. We use these logs to keep the site secure and to fix problems. They are deleted after about 90 days, are never used for marketing and are not shared with third parties, except with the appropriate authorities if we report abuse.
  • Security log: separately, we keep a record of attacks and abuse attempts: IP address, date/time, the requested address and the response code. Only unauthorized access attempts, requests over the rate limit, known vulnerability scans and server errors go into it, not ordinary page views. Records are deleted automatically after 90 days, on the same terms as the server logs.
  • Visitor statistics: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, the pages visited, the referring site and the browser and country they come from. It uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device, and we see only totals, never an individual visitor.

There is no advertising on this site. If we ever add advertising or anything else that uses cookies, this page will be updated first and it will ask for your consent before it runs. See the Cookie Policy for details.

03

How We Use Data

We use your email address only to send the newsletter you subscribed to and the price alerts you asked for. We don't sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising.

Some links to stores may be affiliate links. When you click one and leave our site, the store's own privacy policy and cookies apply there.

04

Who Processes It

Three service providers handle data for us, each only for its own job:

  • Oracle Cloud Infrastructure hosts the site and its database, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Cloudflare carries all traffic to the site (delivery and protection against attacks) and provides the visitor statistics. It runs a worldwide network, and Cloudflare, Inc. is based in the United States.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) delivers the newsletter and alert emails. Your email address goes to Brevo for this purpose only.

Legal Basis

Under the UK GDPR each use of your data needs a legal basis. Ours are:

  • Consent for the newsletter and price alerts. You can withdraw it at any time with the "unsubscribe" link in every email, or by writing to us.
  • Contract for a watchlist recovery link: sending it is the service you asked for.
  • Legitimate interests for server logs, the security log and visitor statistics: keeping the site secure and working, and knowing which pages are read. None of them is used to profile you.

Retention and Deletion

We keep subscription data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every newsletter and alert email has an "unsubscribe" link that deactivates your record immediately. To have your data deleted completely, just write to support@wheyproof.com. Server logs and the security log are deleted after about 90 days.

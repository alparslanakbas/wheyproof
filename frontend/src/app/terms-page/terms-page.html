About These Terms

WheyProof (wheyproof.com) is an independent site that tracks the prices of sports supplements across brand and retailer websites. We don't sell products and we aren't a party to any purchase you make. By using this site you agree to these terms; if you don't agree, please don't use it. How we handle personal data is described in the Privacy Policy.

02

Prices and Product Information

Prices, stock status and product details are collected automatically from each store's own website several times a day. Between our checks a store can change a price, run out of stock or end a promotion, so what you see here may be out of date. The price and terms shown at the store's checkout always apply.

Our "real discount" signals compare the current price with the prices we have recorded ourselves. They describe our own records, not a promise about future prices, and a product we started tracking recently has less history behind its signal.

03

Nutrition Facts

Nutrition facts are copied from the brand's own Nutrition Facts panel, either the text on its product page or its label image, and published only after the calories check out against the protein, carbohydrate and fat on the same panel. A panel can still differ by flavor or package version, and brands change formulas. Always check the label on the product you buy, especially for allergens, which we don't list.

It doesn't change the price you pay, and it doesn't decide which products we list or how deals are ranked: prices and discounts come from the same automatic checks for every store.
